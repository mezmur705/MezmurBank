Privacy Policy

Mezmurify · Last updated September 1, 2026

Mezmurify ("we", "us", "the app") provides a searchable library of Amharic/Ethiopian gospel song lyrics ("Mezmurs"), on the web at mezmurify.com and as a mobile app for Android and iOS. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how you can control it.

Information we collect

You can browse and search the entire song library, view lyrics, and use voice search without creating an account. Signing in with Google is only required to use favorites and comments.

  • Account information. If you sign in with Google, we receive your email address and display name from your Google account, used to create and identify your profile in the app.
  • Content you create. Comments you post on a song (your display name, the comment text, and when you posted it) are stored and shown publicly alongside that song.
  • Your activity, tied to your account. Songs you mark as a favorite, and songs you've recently viewed, are stored against your account so we can show them back to you.
  • Anonymous usage counters. View counts and emoji reactions on songs are stored as simple totals on the song itself - they are not linked to your identity.

Information we do not collect

  • Voice search audio. Voice search uses your device's or browser's own built-in speech recognition to turn speech into search text. The audio itself is processed by your device/browser (or Google's on-device speech service) and is never sent to or stored on Mezmurify's servers - only the resulting search text is used, and only for that search.
  • Your Google Drive. The "Export to Drive" feature saves a song sheet into a shared Google Drive folder that the app's administrators manage for the church/ministry - it uses the app's own administrative Google account, not your personal Google Drive. We never request or receive access to your Drive.

How we use your information

We use the information above only to operate the app's features: signing you in, showing your favorites and recently viewed songs, displaying your comments, and keeping the shared song library up to date. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

App data (accounts, comments, favorites, song library) is stored with Supabase, our database provider. Sign-in is handled by Google. Both providers may process data outside your country as part of providing their service.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data (comments, favorites, recently viewed history) is kept as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below - we'll remove it within 30 days.

Children's privacy

Mezmurify is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
